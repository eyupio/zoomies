# Atmospheric logo backdrops

These three backgrounds implement the approved detailed artwork direction:

- `starlight.webp`: warm clouds, dense stars and constellations (the default).
- `pearl.webp`: flowing pearl and silver mist.
- `orbital.webp`: platinum clouds and luminous blue orbital trails.

They were generated from the reviewed atmospheric concept on 11 October 2026,
with the logo and all lettering removed, then encoded as lossless WebP at the
original resolution. They contain only the background, never a replacement
for the original Zoomies logo. Keep that artwork separate and unchanged.

`Logo.svelte` uses the same detailed asset at every size of the main logo.
Paw icons are excluded and retain their original theme variants without a backdrop. The shared edge mask
feathers the surrounding mist into the page without fading the black logo.
Do not replace these assets with gradients or simplified decorative shapes.
The white square and white circle remain separate, deliberately simple choices.
